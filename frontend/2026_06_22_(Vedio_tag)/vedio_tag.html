HTML Video Tag Attributes Practice

Attributes being used:

  • src – Path to the video
  • width and height – Video dimensions
  • controls – Shows play/pause/volume controls
  • autoplay – Starts automatically
  • muted – Mutes audio
  • loop – Plays repeatedly
  • preload – Hints for loading (auto, metadata, none)
  • poster – Thumbnail shown before play
  • playsinline – Plays inline on mobile devices
